# Scholarly References and Educational Data Mining Bibliography

1. **Cortez, P., & Silva, A. (2008).** *Using data mining to predict secondary school student performance.* In Proceedings of the 5th Annual Future Business Technology Conference (FUBUTEC 2008), pp. 5–12, Porto, Portugal. EUROSIS. ISBN 978-9077381-39-7.  
   *Significance:* Origin paper of the UCI Student Performance benchmark dataset. Established initial evaluations using Random Forests, Support Vector Machines, Decision Trees, and Neural Networks.

2. **Romero, C., & Ventura, S. (2010).** *Educational data mining: a review of the state of the art.* IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, Part C (Applications and Reviews), 40(6), 601–618.  
   *Significance:* Canonical taxonomy of educational data mining applications, including performance modeling, student retention analytics, and feedback systems.

3. **Baker, R. S., & Inventado, P. S. (2014).** *Educational data mining and learning analytics.* In Learning Analytics: From Research to Practice (pp. 61–75). Springer, New York, NY.  
   *Significance:* Provides methodological guidelines on avoiding data leakage and handling longitudinal school records.

4. **Conijn, R., Snijders, C., Kleingeld, A., & Matzat, U. (2017).** *Predicting student performance from LMS data: A comparison of 17 blended courses.* Journal of Educational Technology & Society, 20(1), 17–29.  
   *Significance:* Emphasizes the importance of early formative evaluation markers over late-term metrics for actionable educational interventions.

5. **Lundberg, S. M., & Lee, S. I. (2017).** *A unified approach to interpreting model predictions.* In Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2017), Vol. 30, pp. 4765–4774.  
   *Significance:* Formulates SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) for tree and linear models, providing game-theoretic consistency for individualized instance-level explanations.

6. **Direção-Geral da Educação (DGE). (2012).** *Ensino Secundário: Organização Curricular e Avaliação.* Ministério da Educação e Ciência, Portugal, Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2012.  
   *Significance:* Official statutory curriculum and grading framework defining the 0–20 point evaluation scale and 10-point retention/passing cutoff for Portuguese secondary education.
